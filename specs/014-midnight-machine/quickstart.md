# Quickstart: testing Midnight Machine on flatworld

**Setup:** spec 013 is running (a Lifted Builder), and the client and server have the same test jar.

## M1: music

1. **Plays and fades:** Lift a Builder. The album starts from her. Walk away and it fades, gone by about 24 blocks.
2. **Volume slider:** turn the Jukebox/Note Blocks slider down and the music goes with it.
3. **Pause and resume:** let her land (a meal, or the switch off) and the music stops. When she's Lifted again it resumes within about 5 s of where it was.
4. **Arriving mid-song:** a second player who walks into range mid-track hears the same place.
5. **Restarts:** restart the server mid-track. When she's Lifted again it carries on from about the same place.
6. **Album order:** track 4 (1:36, the shortest) runs into track 5, and track 7 loops back to track 1.

## M2: boombox

1. **Concepts:** the user picks a concept from the renders (the gate before the model).
2. **On her back:** a Lifted Builder wears it, with the album art on the front and the glow visible at night. The cones pulse on the beat. It's gone when she lands.

## M3: dancing

1. **On the beat:** she bobs on the beat and spins on the big hits, through a whole track. Record a clip with the sound if possible (SC-003).
2. **Build speed:** time a job with dancing on and off. The times are within 2% (SC-004).
3. **Music off:** with the server music switch off, she still dances at 100 BPM.
