# Quickstart: Circuit Solver In Game

On the local test server, in creative, with the dev jar on both the client and the server. Carry an Inspoweedometer.

1. **The standard base:** a Hookah, 5 Hemp Wicks and four Snip Stations with rough buds. Light it. All four run at full speed for the minute (time one bud: same as before). The meter on a station: about 4.2 Kb, 25 Ig, 105 W. On the Wick next to the Hookah: about 100 Ig of 200.
2. **Battery:** add a Stash Jar. With the stations idle it charges; take the charcoal out and the stations keep running from the Jar until it's empty.
3. **High:** get high next to the stations. They run, and once their buffers are full they draw nothing: the meter reads 0 W.
4. **Brownout:** put eight stations on one Hookah. All eight run at about half speed, evenly.
5. **Long line:** a 100-Wick Puff line to the four stations. The meter shows about 3% lower voltage at the far end, and the Hookah working a little harder.
6. **Step up:** a Hit Box at each end of the same line, set to Toke, with Rolled Wicks between. The loss on the Wicks about halves.
7. **Burnout by current:** two lit Hookahs and eight stations through one Hemp Wick. That Wick burns out in smoke and ash after a second.
8. **Burnout by insulation:** a Double Hose on a Hemp Wick. The Wick burns.
9. **Overload:** a Double Hose straight onto a Snip Station. The station smokes and stops, keeps its bud, and starts again when the Double Hose is gone.
10. **Old world:** load a copy of the local world saved by 006-willards. Stash charge is the same as before, and nothing overloads.
