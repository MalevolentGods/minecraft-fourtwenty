# Ranks and Stoner Badges: the requirement lists (DRAFT for the user's review)

Format follows the Boy Scout Handbook: numbered requirements with lettered parts. **[T]** = tracked by the game (ticks by itself, with a notice);
**[H]** = on your honour (you tick it and confirm). Every name here is a proposal. Nothing is final until approved.

Rules: ranks are earned in order; badges in any order. Ranks and badges give recognition only (emblem, patch, notice, the sash), no items or boosts.
Colony requirements (marked ⌂) appear only with MineColonies, and no rank needs one.

Tracked share: see the count at the bottom (target: 80% or more).

---

## RANKS

### 1. Tenderbud (Landrace)
*"Every great Weedcrafter started with one confused seed."*
1. [H] Say the Weedcrafter's Oath, out loud if you dare.
2. Grow your first plant.
   - a. [T] Hold a Weed Seed in your hand.
   - b. [T] Plant a seed.
   - c. [T] Harvest a fully grown Landrace plant.
3. Learn to trim and cure.
   - a. [T] Trim a Rough Bud.
   - b. [T] Cure a Trimmed Bud.
4. Smoke.
   - a. [T] Roll a Weed Joint.
   - b. [T] Smoke it.
5. [H] Explain to a friend why you always keep one seed back.

*"The first rung off the ground. Mind the ladder."*

4. [T] Place a Hookah and light it.

7. [T] Earn the **Ganja Gardening** badge.
8. [T] Earn any one other Stoner Badge.
9. [H] Show someone how to read a Wick with an Inspoweedometer.
