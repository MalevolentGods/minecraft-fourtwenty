# Quickstart: The Tie Dye Shack in game

On the local test server, with the College's Chemistry tier 1 (`/fourtwenty college finish fourtwenty:chem_neutrals`, and the others as needed) and Crumple (`…finish fourtwenty:tex_crumple`) done.

## M1: the Shack and tie-dyed hemp
1. Build a Tie Dye Shack level 1 (a vat and a rack inside), hire a Tie Dyer.
2. In its Orders tab, order 8 Crumple hemp in Ashtray. Other patterns show "Needs the study", colourways with unresearched colours show what's missing.
3. Stock the warehouse with Natural Hemp, Cured Buds and Glass Bottles. Watch: requests, dip at the vat, hang at the rack, the blocks arrive in the warehouse.
4. Place them: Crumple in black, grey and white. Mine and re-place: unchanged. Save, quit, reload: unchanged.
5. As a player who hasn't caught grey: the swatch shows "?", the item doesn't name it, and your caught colours are unchanged.

## M2: rugs, couches, beds
Order each; place rugs side by side (same look joins, a different colourway doesn't); sit on a couch; sleep in a bed.

## M3: shirts
Hand a shirt to a colonist; see it worn from another player's view; swap it; take it back; reload.

## M4: patterns, levels and the hut
All 8 Textiles studies done: all 8 patterns at a glance in a showcase row. Build levels 1-5; a second Tie Dyer from level 3; the hut block.

## M5: the player's route and the book
The Dye Vat without MineColonies (if approved). The Handbook page. Four Twenty starts without MineColonies.
