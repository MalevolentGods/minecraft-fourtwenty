title: Pot Pots

terms: [Pot Pot, Potted Pot Pot]
---
A **Pot Pot** is a flowerpot for weed, in any of the hemp colours: a grow you can put on a windowsill.

1. Right-click a Pot Pot with a seed. It becomes a **Potted Pot Pot** with a sprout.
2. Keep it in the light. It grows through the same eight stages as a crop in the field, and bone meal hurries it.
3. When it's mature, right-click it to harvest: you get the crop's buds and seed, and the plant comes out, leaving the empty pot ready for the next seed.

Breaking a Potted Pot Pot gives it back with its plant, so you can move your grow without losing it.
