{item:fourtwenty:weed_seeds|Weed Seeds. Every grow starts here.}

A Weedcrafter's first seeds are **Landrace**: plain, honest, everyday weed, the kind that grows wild.

# Wild Weed
Wild weed grows in the open country: **plains, savannas, meadows and groves**. Look for tall, leafy plants among the grass. Break one and it drops a seed, and an older, taller plant can drop two or three. A good scout gathers a handful before heading home.

# Starter Seeds
If you play with MineColonies, you're given **four Landrace seeds** the first time you join, ready for your colony's first garden. Spend them wisely: until you learn to trim, seeds are precious.

# Keep Some Back
A seed in the pocket is worth two in the bong. However hungry you are for your first smoke, always keep one seed back to plant again. You'll learn how to win more seeds in [Seeds and Where They Come From](entry:growing/seeds).
