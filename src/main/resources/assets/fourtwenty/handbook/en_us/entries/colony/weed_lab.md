The **Weed Lab** is a building that grows with you: a processing and research facility whose machines are replaced, level by level, with the next tier's. Your Builder puts up the whole setup for you, so long as you've crafted the blocks it needs. As it levels it adds Grow Ops, a Think Tank, Pheno machines, trimmers and curers, a Rosin Rig, powered crafting benches and Re-Up Stations, all run from its own Hookah and Stash, and from level 4 its Terpinator and Infuser are the Lab models.

Each level wires its power the proper way, ready to run the moment it's built.
