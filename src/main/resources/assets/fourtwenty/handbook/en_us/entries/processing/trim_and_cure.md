The **Trim Tray** and **Curing Rack** need no power and no fuss. Every Weedcrafter starts here, and some never leave.

{image:scenes/trim_and_cure|A hand bench: Trim Tray, Curing Rack, and somewhere to keep the buds|wide}

{recipe:fourtwenty:trim_tray}

Right-click the Trim Tray with Rough Buds: each click snips one into a Trimmed Bud, and sometimes drops a seed of its strain (see [Seeds and Where They Come From](entry:growing/seeds)).

{recipe:fourtwenty:curing_rack}

Right-click the Curing Rack with Trimmed Buds to hang up to eight. In five minutes they're **Cured Buds**. An empty hand takes them down.

When you're harvesting more than you can snip by hand, and your snipping hand has started to cramp, it's time for [Trimmers and Curers](entry:processing/machines).
