A **Stash** is a battery. Its voltage rises as it fills, so a lit Hookah charges it with whatever the machines don't need, and when the line drops below it, the Stash runs them instead. Either way, it moves at most a Hookah's current of its tier.

{table}
| Stash | Tier | Stores |
| Stash Jar | Puff | 252,000 Ws |
| Stash Box | Toke | 2,520,000 Ws |
| Mother Stash | Rip | 25,200,000 Ws |
| The Vault | Blaze | 252,000,000 Ws |
{/table}

Connect one anywhere on a Wick line. Two Stashes on one line level out, the fuller one topping up the other. Break the block and everything stored in it is gone. A full Vault holds 252 million Willard-seconds, and every one of them can be lost to a single absent-minded swing of a pickaxe.

How long a Stash will run your machines is a sum, and it's in [Power, Energy and Heat](entry:electronics/energy).
