A **Stash** is a battery. Its voltage rises as it fills, so a lit Hookah charges it with whatever the machines don't need, and when the line drops below it, it runs them. Either way it moves at most a Hookah's current of its tier.

{table}
| Stash | Tier | Stores |
| Stash Jar | Puff | 252,000 Ws |
| Stash Box | Toke | 2,520,000 Ws |
| Mother Stash | Rip | 25,200,000 Ws |
| The Vault | Blaze | 252,000,000 Ws |
{/table}

Connect one anywhere on a Wick line. Two Stashes on a line level out, the fuller one charging the other. Stored BI is lost if you break the block, so mind your pickaxe.
