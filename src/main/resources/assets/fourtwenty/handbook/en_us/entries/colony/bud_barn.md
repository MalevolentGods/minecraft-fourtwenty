Colony farmers grow weed like any crop, but their harvest comes in as **Rough Buds**, and a Dispensary only takes **Cured Buds**. The **Bud Barn** closes the gap: its workers trim and cure the colony's harvest at its trays and racks, keep the rough and trimmed buds, and send cured buds and seeds to the warehouse.

Without one, the colony's buds wait for you to trim and cure them by hand, and the Dispensary runs dry in the meantime.
