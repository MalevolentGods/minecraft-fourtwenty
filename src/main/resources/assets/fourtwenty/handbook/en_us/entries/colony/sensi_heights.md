"A hillside town that grows around the grow." **Sensi Heights** is a MineColonies style that comes with Four Twenty: every hut, from the Town Hall to the Weed Lab, in the mod's own palette of coloured concrete, hemp and Domum Ornamentum. Choose it in the Build Tool with Switch Pack.

Start your colony from its **Supply Camp** or **Supply Ship**: their racks are stocked for a fresh start, with a few weed seeds and a copy of this Handbook.

Buildings grow fancier as they level up: from level 3 they wear the town's signature door, and at level 5 every hut has lamps in its own colour.
