When a weed plant stands full-grown and flowering, it's ready. Right-click it (or break it) to harvest.

A ripe plant gives:

- **Rough Buds** of its strain: one for Landrace, more for better strains (up to five for Golden God),
- and its **seed** back, to plant again.

The whole plant comes up with the harvest, just as a real one would, so the ground is left bare. Replant straight away and the garden never sleeps.

An unripe plant, picked too soon, gives only its seed back. Patience, Weedcrafter.

# Big Harvests
The two finest Grow Ops, the **Aero Pod** and the **Cosmic Kush Pod**, give **half as many again** of every bud a plant yields. Some plots also shake loose an extra seed now and then.

# What Next?
Rough buds need trimming and curing before they're any good. If you skipped it, read [Your First Smoke](entry:getting_started/first_smoke).
