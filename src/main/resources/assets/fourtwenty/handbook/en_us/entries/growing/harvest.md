When a weed plant stands full-grown and flowering, it's ready. Right-click it (or break it) to harvest.

A ripe plant gives:

- **Rough Buds** of its strain: one for Landrace, more for better strains (up to five for Golden God),
- and its **seed** back, to plant again.

The whole plant comes up with the harvest, roots and all, so the ground is left bare. Replant straight away.

An unripe plant, picked too soon, gives only its seed back. You'll do this at least once, out of pure impatience. So did we.

# Big Harvests
The two finest Grow Ops, the **Aero Pod** and the **Cosmic Kush Pod**, give **half as many again** of every bud a plant yields. Some plots also shake loose an extra seed now and then.

# What Next?
Rough Buds need trimming and curing before they're any good. If you skipped that part, it's in [Your First Smoke](entry:getting_started/first_smoke).
