Weed wants three things from you: wet farmland, good light, and time.

# Light
A weed plant grows only in light of **level 9 or brighter**. Sunshine does nicely by day; at night, or indoors, put a lamp close by. A plant in the dark doesn't wilt or die. It just stops growing until you sort the light out.

# Time
Weed grows at about **half the pace** of ordinary crops, and the rarer strains are slower still: a Golden God grows at a quarter of a Landrace's pace. Weedcrafters with big plans keep more than one crop on the go.

# Bone Meal
Bone meal hurries along everyday weed (Landrace and the first families). The rarer hybrids won't take it: they grow on their own schedule and accept no bribes.

# Grow Ops
Serious growers plant in **Grow Ops**: plots built to grow weed faster. The humble **Closet Grow** needs only light and grows half again as fast. Beyond it come the **Grow Tent**, **Hydro Bed**, **Aero Pod** and **Cosmic Kush Pod**, each quicker than the last, and each wanting power. The rarest strains won't grow in plain dirt at all. See [Grow Ops](entry:growing/grow_ops).
