# Feeding Them
Put buds in on the left and take the results from the right. Better still, feed them with **hoppers** from the top or sides and empty them with a hopper underneath, or join them up with Transport Tubes, and you never touch a bud again until it's ready to smoke. A trimmer's seeds come out with its buds.

# Power
Run Wicks from a Hookah or Stash of the **same tier**, or stand nearby while high and the machine runs for free. With neither, it pauses and keeps its place.

A source of a higher tier **overloads** a machine: it stops work and stands there smoking, like it's on its break, until you take that source away. Nothing gets damaged. To run a lower-tier machine from a big source, step the power down with a [Hit Box](entry:power/hit_box).
