The **Rosin Rig** refines Cured Buds into smaller, stronger things, one step at a time. It runs on Toke power, or free near a high Weedcrafter.

{table}
| In | Out |
| 1 Cured Bud | 1 Kief |
| 2 Kief | 1 Hash |
| 1 Hash | 1 Resin |
| 1 Resin | 1 Rosin, or 1 Oil with a Glass Bottle |
{/table}

# What They're For
In a **Hookah**, one charcoal burns Kief for 2 minutes, Hash for 5, Resin for 7½, and Rosin or Oil for 15: long burns for long sessions and steady power. Biome strains burn half as long again. In a **pipe**, Hash and up smoke stronger and longer. **Oil** loads the [Vape Pen](entry:processing/vape_pen).

Extracts keep the strain and traits of the bud. They don't make terpenes: those come from the [Terpinator](entry:terpinator/using).
