Every circuit here is built on the same simple **board**, so build it once and keep it.

1. Place a **Hookah** and fill it: a few Cured Buds, a water bucket and some charcoal. It lights by itself whenever something on its wires needs power.
2. Run a row of **red Wicks** away from it in a straight line. This is the **+ rail**.
3. Run a row of **black Wicks** away from it in a parallel line, two blocks over. This is the **− rail**.
4. Put the Hookah in the middle between them, so its + side touches the red row and its − side the black.

Red is plus and black is minus: power flows only around a closed loop, out along red and back along black.

# Power for a Nug
Every logic part (a **Nug**) needs two power connections: **VDD** on its **top** and **GND** on its **bottom**. Run a red Wick from the + rail to the block above the Nug, and a black Wick from the − rail to the block below it. Colour matters: a Wick joins only Wicks of its own colour.

# Seeing It Work
A Nug glows on its face while its output is 1. A **Lit Bit** glows white while it has power. To check a reading, hold an Inspoweedometer and look at it.

# If Nothing Happens
- Is the Hookah full? (A bud, water, charcoal.)
- Does the red line touch the Hookah's + side?
- Is there a **loop**? Power needs a way home.
- Did a Wick burn out? It smokes and leaves ash. Replace it.
