title: Circuit 1: A Switched Light
icon: fourtwenty:toggle_joint
terms: [Toggle Joint, switch]
---
**The goal:** a light with a switch. Flip it and the light comes on. It's the first circuit anyone builds, and still the most useful.

**You'll need:** your [workbench](entry:electronics/board), a **Toggle Joint**, a **Lit Bit** and a few Wicks.

1. On the board, stop the red rail one block short of its end.
2. Put a **Toggle Joint** in that gap, facing along the rail, so its two pins line up with the rail.
3. Carry a red Wick on from the switch's far side to a **Lit Bit**.
4. Run the black rail on to the Lit Bit's other side.
5. Flip the switch.

**What happens:** with the switch open, the loop is broken and the light stays dark. Closed, the loop is complete: power flows from the Hookah, through the switch and the Lit Bit, and home along the black rail. The Lit Bit glows.

**The real thing:** this is a light switch in your wall, exactly.
