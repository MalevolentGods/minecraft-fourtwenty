Edibles are food that takes a while to kick in. Patience is a virtue.

{recipe:fourtwenty:weed_cookie}
The **Weed Cookie** kicks in after 10 seconds and lasts about 3 minutes: Regeneration, Night Vision, Luck and the munchies.

{recipe:fourtwenty:weed_brownie}
The **Weed Brownie** kicks in after 15 seconds and lasts about 5 minutes. Stronger, and it adds Speed and Jump Boost.

# Good to Know
- You can't eat another until the first one kicks in.
- Logging out or dying before then cancels it.
