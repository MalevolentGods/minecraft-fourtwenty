You eat it. Nothing happens for ten, maybe fifteen seconds. You start to suspect you've been had. Then it happens.

{recipe:fourtwenty:weed_cookie}
The **Weed Cookie** kicks in after 10 seconds and lasts about 3 minutes: Regeneration, Night Vision, Luck, and the munchies, which is a bold side effect for something that's already food.

{recipe:fourtwenty:weed_brownie}
The **Weed Brownie** kicks in after 15 seconds and lasts about 5 minutes. It's stronger, and adds Speed and Jump Boost, so you can get to the next brownie faster.

# Good to Know
- You can't eat a second one until the first kicks in. This rule exists because of a Weedcrafter we'll call Dennis.
- Die, or call it a day, before it kicks in, and it's wasted.
