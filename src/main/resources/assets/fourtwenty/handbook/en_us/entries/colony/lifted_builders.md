Turn on **Fly when Lifted** in a Builder's hut, keep spare joints in the colony, and your Builder will smoke one to become **Lifted**: she flies, builds from the air, and is never stranded on a roof.

While she's Lifted she wears the **Midnight Machine**, a boombox backpack playing the album *Midnight Machine*. Everyone nearby hears it, and she dances to the beat as she works. A colony's Builders share one playlist, so a busy site sounds like one party, not a fight between speakers. The music follows the Jukebox and Note Blocks volume slider.
