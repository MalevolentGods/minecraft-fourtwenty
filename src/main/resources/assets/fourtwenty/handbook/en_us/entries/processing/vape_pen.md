{recipe:fourtwenty:vape_pen}

The tidiest smoke there is, and the longest-lasting one that fits in a pocket. Load it with **Oil**: in your inventory, right-click the Oil onto the pen. One Oil is **8 hits**, each a full high, and the pen holds **16**.

It needs no power, no charcoal and no water. Make Oil at the [Rosin Rig](entry:processing/extracts).
