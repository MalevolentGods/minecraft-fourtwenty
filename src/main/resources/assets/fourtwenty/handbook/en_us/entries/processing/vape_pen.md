A tidy smoke that lasts. Load it with **Oil**: in your inventory, right-click the Oil onto the pen. One Oil is **8 hits**, each a full high, and the pen holds **16**.

It's the longest-lasting smoke in your pocket, and it needs no power. Make Oil at the [Rosin Rig](entry:processing/extracts).
