**Power** is how fast work gets done. **Energy** is how much work gets done altogether. Willards (W) measure power; **Willard-seconds (Ws)** measure energy: one Willard for one second is one Ws, which the rest of the world calls a **joule**.

Mixing the two up is the most common mistake in all of electronics. It's the difference between how fast you can eat and how much food there is.

# Two Sums
- **energy = power × time.** A Puff Hookah gives 420 W for a 60-second burn: 420 × 60 = **25,200 Ws**, the figure in its table.
- **time = energy ÷ power.** A full Stash Jar holds 252,000 Ws, ten burns' worth. A Puff machine takes 105 W, so a full Jar runs one for 252,000 ÷ 105 = 2,400 seconds: **40 minutes**. Four machines empty it in 10.

# Where It All Goes
Energy is never made or destroyed, only moved around and changed into something else. A Hookah doesn't *make* BI: it changes weed into it. Where the weed got it from is a question for [A Short History of Boundless Inspiration](entry:power/history), which doesn't know either.

Every Willard that goes into a Resinator comes out as heat. That's the whole job of a Resinator: it turns push into warmth, on purpose. A Wick does the same thing a little per block, by accident, which is why long lines lose power. A Hit Box keeps 2% of whatever passes through it, also as heat.

# Efficiency
**Efficiency** is what comes out ÷ what goes in. A Hit Box is 98% efficient; two in a row, 98% × 98% = 96%. Every step in a chain takes its cut, which is why good designs use as few steps as possible, and why bad ones run warm.
