Every seed carries three **traits**, each rated **1 to 5**. Look at a seed, bud or joint and its label reads *Potency a, Yield b, Length c*. They belong to the **seed**, not to the strain: two Cherry Pie seeds can be as different as two siblings.

# What Each Does

{table}
| Rating | Potency (effect levels) | Yield (buds a harvest) | Length (high is) |
| 1 | -1 | -1 | 1x, plain |
| 2 | plain | plain | 1.5x |
| 3 | +1 | +1 | 2x |
| 4 | +1 | +2 | 2.5x |
| 5 | +2 | +3 | 3x |
{/table}

- **Potency** raises the level of the strain's effects (a Regeneration I becomes II). A smoke can never be pushed above level IV by traits and family together.
- **Yield** adds rough buds to the harvest. A plant always gives at least one.
- **Length** stretches the high and every effect in it. A Length 5 seed gives a high three times as long as a Length 1 seed of the same strain.

Potency and Yield 2 are plain. For Length, **1 is plain**: a seed with Length 1 gives exactly the high this book says.

# Where Traits Come From
- **Landrace**, and every strain you infuse from it, is plain: **Potency 2, Yield 2, Length 1.** Nothing is random about this.
- **Only crossing changes traits.** Cross two seeds in a Pheno Chamber, or let a Buzz Bot or the wind pollinate two plants, and the child's traits are a mix of its parents'.
- **A plant passes its traits on** to everything it gives: its replant seed, its Rough Buds, and the seeds you trim from them.
- **Extracts** add to a pipe load's Potency and Length for as long as it smokes: Hash +1, Resin, Rosin and Oil +2, never past 5. Your seed is unchanged.

# How a Child's Traits Are Decided
Each trait is worked out on its own:

1. Take the **average** of the two parents. If it falls between two ratings (a 2 and a 3), the child gets either one, half and half.
2. Then it **drifts**: 1 in 4 it drops by one, 1 in 4 it rises by one, and half the time it stays put.
3. It never goes below 1 or above 5.

So a pair of 3s have 1-in-4 children that are 4s, and 1-in-4 that are 2s. The way up is slow and unglamorous: keep the best child of every cross, and cross it with the best of the next. Plant breeders have always done it this way, and not one of them has ever called it exciting.

# Breeding for Traits
The Pheno Chamber can also cross two seeds of the **same** strain: the child is that strain again, with mixed traits. That is how you breed Length and Potency without changing strain. The higher the grade of the seed, the better the chamber you need: **Toke** power up to Crosses, a **Pheno Lab** (Rip) for Double Crosses, a **Pheno Cathedral** (Blaze) for Ultras and Golden God. See [Breeding](entry:growing/breeding).

Strains with a **family potency** are boosted on top of their traits: see [How a High Works](entry:processing/highs).
