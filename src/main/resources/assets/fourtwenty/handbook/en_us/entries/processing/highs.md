Smoke any strain and you are **high**: the edges of your sight take the strain's colour, your smoke drifts in it, and the strain's effects run. A high doesn't wear off while you're away: it picks up where it left off when you come back.

# How Long It Lasts
The strain's **grade** sets the base length of the high (the colour tint, the aura, and the time machines count you as high):

{table}
| Grade | Family | Base high |
| 0 | Landrace | 2 minutes |
| 1 | Neutrals, Warm, Green, Cool, Biome | 3 minutes |
| 2 | Cross | 4 minutes |
| 3 | Double Cross | 5 minutes |
| 4 | Ultra | 6 minutes |
| 5 | Golden God | 5 minutes |
{/table}

Then the seed's **Length** trait stretches it: x1 at Length 1, up to x3 at Length 5 (see [Traits](entry:growing/traits)). A Length 5 Ultra is high for 18 minutes. A few strains have a length of their own (Purple Haze 5 minutes, Third Eye Tonic 90 seconds).

# How Long Each Effect Lasts
Each strain's effects have their own lengths, set by the strain: Landrace's Regeneration lasts 30 seconds, its Night Vision 2 minutes. Every one of them is stretched by the same **Length** trait, and by the family's **potency** (below). So the tint and the effects you feel can last different lengths of time. A strain whose only effect is short will look short, however long the tint stays.

# How Strong It Is
An effect starts at the strain's own level, then gains:

- the **seed's Potency**: -1, 0, +1, +1, +2 levels for ratings 1 to 5;
- the **family's potency**: a rating of its own, set by the family. At the default balance, Landrace 1; Neutrals, Warm and Green 2; Cool, Biome and Cross 3; Double Cross and Ultra 4; Golden God 5.

{table}
| Family potency | Extra effect levels | Effects last |
| 1 | none | x1.0 |
| 2 | none | x1.1 |
| 3 | +1 | x1.2 |
| 4 | +1 | x1.3 |
| 5 | +2 | x1.4 |
{/table}

The boost can never lift an effect above **level IV**. A strain that already starts higher keeps its own level.

# Other Ways to Smoke
- **A pipe** with extracts adds +1 (Hash) or +2 (Resin, Rosin, Oil) to Potency and Length for as long as the load smokes. Kief adds nothing.
- **A bong** gives the same high, and its **specialty** versions add a buff for a whole day.
- **A Hookah** gives everyone within 4 blocks a mellow high while it burns, and powers your machines.
- **Edibles** have their own timings: see [Edibles](entry:processing/edibles).

# While You Are High
Machines within **21 blocks** of a high Weedcrafter run free, with no power needed. Smoke again while still high and machines count you as high until the later of the two ends. Smoking also inspires colours for the [Inspiration Infuser](entry:colours/infuser).

# Why Is My High Short?
Check the seed's label. A seed with **Length 1** gets exactly the base time in the table. If you cross better ones you will see it grow. If a strain's effects feel short, it is the strain's own effect length, not a bug: try another strain, or smoke from a better-bred seed.
