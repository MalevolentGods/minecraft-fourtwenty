You hold in your hands the collected wisdom of generations of Weedcrafters: how to find a seed, grow it, trim it, cure it, and smoke it with your friends. Further in, it will teach you to research colours, run a Terpinator, power a whole workshop on the smoke of a Hookah, and even build working circuits.

Nobody learns all of it in a day. Read a little, try a little, and come back when you're ready for more. The first rule of this book is printed inside its cover, and it is a good one.

# How the Work Goes
Every Weedcrafter's day follows the same honest path:

1. **Grow** weed from seed in good light.
2. **Trim** the rough buds you harvest.
3. **Cure** the trimmed buds until they're ready.
4. **Smoke**, share, and enjoy.

Everything else in this book grows out of those four steps. Turn the page to find your first seeds.
