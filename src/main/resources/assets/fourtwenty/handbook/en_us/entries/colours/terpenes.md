Every colour in the Four Twenty world comes from a **terpene**, a little vial of essence made from weed. Terpenes dye hemp blocks, breed new strains, and give each strain its smell and its colour.

# The Four Families
Terpenes come in four families, each covering a sweep of the rainbow:

- **Whites, creams, greys and blacks.**
- **Browns, yellows, reds and oranges.**
- **Olives, limes, greens and teals.**
- **Purples, pinks, blues and skies.**

A terpene blends three parts: one sets the family, one the hue, and one the tone, light to dark. Sixty-four hemp colours in all, plus the sixteen Minecraft dyes.

# Discovery
You start not knowing any of them. A terpene you haven't met shows no name, and this book won't spoil it for you. You learn a colour by **catching** it at the [Inspiration Infuser](entry:colours/infuser), or by making or receiving its vial.

# Using Them
Surround a terpene vial with eight hemp blocks in a crafting grid to dye them. Feed terpenes and a Landrace seed to the Infuser to breed a strain.
