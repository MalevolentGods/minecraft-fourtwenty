New strains are bred, not found. Breeding starts in a **Pheno Chamber** and climbs from there.

# The Pheno Chamber
Put a seed in each slot. The right two strains make a **Cross**, and the chamber tells you which. Any other pair gives you one of the parents back, which is the chamber's polite way of saying no. It runs on Toke power, or free near a high Weedcrafter.

# Climate Seeds
Landrace grown in a **desert, snow, swamp or jungle** sometimes gives a climate-touched seed. Put one in the Pheno Chamber on its own and it settles into that biome's strain.

# Traits
Every seed has **Potency**, **Yield** and **Length**, each rated 1 to 5. A new seed takes its parents' average, then may move one up or down. Cross your best seeds to breed better ones. The full rules, and what each rating does, are in [Traits](entry:growing/traits).

# Above the Crosses
Double Crosses, Ultras and Golden God can't be made in a chamber. Grow two Double Cross parents side by side on good plots, and now and then a harvest gives the seed, the old-fashioned way.

{item:fourtwenty:buzz_bot|The Buzz Bot, a robot bee}

A **Buzz Bot** takes the luck out of it. One Cured Bud runs it for an hour, and every five minutes or so it pollinates ripe crops that can cross, for a guaranteed hybrid at the next harvest. Ultras and Golden God need one.

# Better Traits Higher Up
The **Pheno Lab** (Rip) breeds better traits into Double Crosses, and the **Pheno Cathedral** (Blaze) into Ultras and Golden God.
