**Boundless Inspiration**, BI for short, is the power that runs a Weedcrafter's machines. It's made by burning weed in a Hookah, stored in Stashes, and carried along Wicks.

# Tiers
Power comes in four tiers, each ten times the voltage of the last:

{table}
| Tier | Voltage | Hookah | Wick |
| Puff | 4.2 Kb | Hookah | Hemp Wick |
| Toke | 42 Kb | Double Hose | Rolled Wick |
| Rip | 420 Kb | Big Bertha | Glass Wick |
| Blaze | 4,200 Kb | Grand Hookah | Gold Wick |
{/table}

Every machine has a tier. Feed it from a source of the **same** tier. A higher tier overloads it: it stops and smokes until you remove that source, and never breaks.

# Running Free
Most machines need no power at all while a **high Weedcrafter is within 21 blocks**. Inspiration really is a kind of power. When nobody's high, they draw BI; with neither, they pause and keep their place.

# Units
The meter reads in Weedcraft's own units: Kerberos (Kb) for voltage, Ignus (Ig) for current, Willards (W) for power. The real-world names are at the back of the book.
