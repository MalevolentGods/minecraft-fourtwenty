You now own a handful of Rough Buds and a strong urge to set fire to them. Hold that thought. A Rough Bud is sticky, leafy, and about as smokeable as a lettuce. Two small jobs stand between it and a joint: **trim**, then **cure**.

# Trim
Build a **Trim Tray** and put it somewhere you don't mind getting sticky.

{recipe:fourtwenty:trim_tray}

Right-click it with Rough Buds in your hand: each click snips one into a **Trimmed Bud**. Now and then a seed drops out of the trimmings. Pick it up. Nearly every seed you'll ever own comes out of trimmings.

# Cure
Build a **Curing Rack**.

{recipe:fourtwenty:curing_rack}

Right-click it with Trimmed Buds to hang them up, eight at most, then wait five minutes. You will find this harder than it sounds. Take them down with an empty hand.

{item:fourtwenty:weed_bud|A Cured Bud. This is the one you smoke.}

# Smoke
Roll a Cured Bud between two sheets of paper:

{recipe:fourtwenty:weed_joint}

Hold the joint and use it. Somewhere, an unbroken line of Weedcrafters, stretching back to the first person who looked at a plant and thought *I bet that burns*, gives you one small, approving nod. You've done it. Everything after this is doing it better.
