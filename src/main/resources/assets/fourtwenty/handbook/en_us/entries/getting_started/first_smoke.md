Between the plant and the joint lie two small jobs that every Weedcrafter learns by heart: **trim**, then **cure**.

# Trim
Build a **Trim Tray** and set it down somewhere handy.

{recipe:fourtwenty:trim_tray}

Right-click it with Rough Buds in your hand: each click snips one into a **Trimmed Bud**. Now and then a seed falls out of the trimmings, so keep an eye on the tray.

# Cure
Build a **Curing Rack**.

{recipe:fourtwenty:curing_rack}

Right-click it with Trimmed Buds to hang them up, up to eight at a time. In five minutes they're cured. Take them down with an empty hand.

{item:fourtwenty:weed_bud|A Cured Bud: ready to smoke and craft with}

# Smoke
Roll a joint from a Cured Bud between two sheets of paper:

{recipe:fourtwenty:weed_joint}

Hold it and use it to smoke. Congratulations, Weedcrafter. You've done it.
