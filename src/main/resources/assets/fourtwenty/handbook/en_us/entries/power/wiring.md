Power flows only around a **closed loop**: out from a source, through a machine, and back. If it can't get home, it doesn't leave.

1. Run a **red** Wick from the source's + to the machine (red is +).
2. Run a **black** Wick back to the source (black is −).

A Wick connects on any side of a block. Dye Wicks other colours to keep lines apart: a Wick joins only Wicks of **its own colour**, so two lines in different colours can run side by side and never meet.

# Cables
A **Twin Wick** is a two-core cable that wires every block it touches across it, + to + and − to −, in **parallel**. A **Daisy Wick** wires the blocks along it one after another, in **series**, and closes the loop itself. It can't branch.

# Ratings
Each Wick is rated for its tier: too much current, or a higher tier's voltage, and it **burns out** in a puff of smoke, leaving ash. Everyone burns out a Wick sooner or later. There's a badge for it. A Wick carries about two Hookahs of its tier.

| Wick | Tier | Current | Insulated to |
| Hemp | Puff | 200 Ig | 6.3 Kb |
| Rolled | Toke | 40 Ig | 63 Kb |
| Glass | Rip | 8 Ig | 630 Kb |
| Gold | Blaze | 1.6 Ig | 6,300 Kb |
