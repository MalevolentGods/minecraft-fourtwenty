Hold right-click to smoke. Every smoke gives its strain's high.

# Joints
{recipe:fourtwenty:weed_joint}
Two papers and a bud along the bottom row. One smoke, then it's gone.

# Pipes
{recipe:fourtwenty:weed_pipe}
A pipe holds up to 16 buds, one per smoke. Hash and stronger extracts smoke stronger and longer in it.

# Bongs
{recipe:fourtwenty:glass_bong}
Place a bong anywhere; its bowl turns to face you, which is either good manners or slightly unsettling. Right-click it with a Cured Bud in your inventory to take a hit: one bud a hit, with bubbles and smoke, and five seconds between hits.

**Specialty bongs** add a buff that lasts a whole day: craft a glass bong with two ingredients.

{table}
| Bong | Add | Buff |
| Jackrabbit | rabbit's foot, sugar | Speed II, Jump Boost II |
| Deep Sea | prismarine crystals, nautilus shell | Water Breathing, Dolphin's Grace, Conduit Power |
| Forge | blaze rod, magma cream | Fire Resistance, Strength |
| Owl | golden carrot, spyglass | Night Vision, Haste II |
| Sage | amethyst shard, book | Luck II, Hero of the Village |
| Phantom | phantom membrane, feather | Flight for the day |
{/table}

# The Hookah
A shared smoke: everyone within 4 blocks gets a mellow high while it burns, including people who were only walking past. Give it buds, a water bucket and charcoal; each burn uses one bud and one charcoal and lasts a minute. A Hookah is also a power source: see [Hookahs](entry:power/hookahs).
