A Grow Op is a plot that grows any farmland crop faster and keeps it watered. Right-click one with seeds to plant; right-click with an empty hand to see how it's doing.

{table}
| Plot | Speed | Power |
| Closet Grow | 1.5x | none, needs light |
| Grow Tent | 2x | Puff |
| Hydro Bed | 3x | Toke |
| Aero Pod | 5x | Rip |
| Cosmic Kush Pod | 8x | Blaze |
{/table}

{image:scenes/grow_ops|The ladder of plots, from the Closet Grow to the Cosmic Kush Pod|wide}

# Upgrading
Use the next tier's plot on the one you have: the crop stays right where it is.

# Power
From the Grow Tent up, a plot runs on BI through Wicks, or free while a high Weedcrafter is within 21 blocks. Without power the crop simply grows at the normal pace and comes to no harm. Green sparkles show each boost.

# Big Harvests
The Aero Pod and Cosmic Kush Pod give half as many again of every bud. Other crops replant themselves there; weed, like a real plant, comes up with the harvest.

# Climates
A Cosmic Kush Pod can copy a climate: right-click it with sand, a snow block, a lily pad or a jungle sapling. Landrace grown in a climate sometimes gives a climate-touched seed (see [Breeding](entry:growing/breeding)).

# Which Strains Grow Where
The best strains are fussy. A Cross needs at least a Closet Grow; a Double Cross a Hydro Bed; an Ultra an Aero Pod; and Golden God nothing less than a Cosmic Kush Pod.
