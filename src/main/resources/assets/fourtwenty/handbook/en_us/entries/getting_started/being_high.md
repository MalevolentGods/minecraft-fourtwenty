Smoking gets you **high**. The edges of your sight take on the strain's colour, your smoke drifts out in that colour too, and for a while you feel the strain's effects.

Plain Landrace is a kind smoke:

- **Regeneration**, to mend your scrapes.
- **Night Vision**, so you can see exactly what's coming for you in the dark.
- **Luck** on your side.
- And a touch of **Hunger**. Keep something to eat nearby. You'll want it.

How long a high lasts, and how strong it is, is explained in [How a High Works](entry:processing/highs). Other strains do other things, and the rare ones do things you'll want witnesses for. You'll meet them in [Strains, Families and Grades](entry:growing/strains).

# Sesh Courtesy
Smoke with friends, and pass to the left. If you're not sure which way left is, you've had enough. Don't smoke anywhere you'd be embarrassed to be found, and never leave a lit Hookah unattended.

# Useful, Too
Being high is also, of all things, useful: machines within 21 blocks of a high Weedcrafter run without any power at all. More on that in [Boundless Inspiration](entry:power/basics).
