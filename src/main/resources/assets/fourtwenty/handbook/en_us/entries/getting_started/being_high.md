Smoking gives you a **high**. The edges of your sight take on the strain's colour, your smoke drifts in that colour too, and you feel the strain's effects for a while.

Plain Landrace is a good, kind smoke:

- **Regeneration** to mend your scrapes.
- **Night Vision**, so the dark holds no fear.
- **Luck** on your side.
- And a touch of **Hunger**. The munchies are real. Pack a snack.

How long it lasts, and how strongly, is explained in [How a High Works](entry:processing/highs). Other strains do other things, and the rarer ones do remarkable things. You'll meet them in [Strains, Families and Grades](entry:growing/strains).

# Sesh Courtesy
Smoke with friends and pass to the left. Don't smoke where you'd be ashamed to be found. And never, ever leave a Hookah burning unattended.

A high Weedcrafter is good for more than giggles: later in this book you'll find machines that run for free while someone nearby is high. That's no accident. Inspiration is a kind of power.
