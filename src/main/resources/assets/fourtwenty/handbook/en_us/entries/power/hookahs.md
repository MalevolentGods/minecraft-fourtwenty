A **Hookah** is a shared smoke and a power source in one, which makes it the only generator anyone has ever been glad to sit next to. It needs a Cured Bud, a water bucket and charcoal; each burn uses a bud and a charcoal and lasts a minute. It lights itself when something on its Wicks needs power, and only then.

{table}
| Hookah | Tier | Power | Per burn |
| Hookah | Puff | 420 W | 25,200 Ws |
| Double Hose | Toke | 840 W | 50,400 Ws |
| Big Bertha | Rip | 1,680 W | 100,800 Ws |
| Grand Hookah | Blaze | 3,360 W | 201,600 Ws |
{/table}

A burn runs **four machines** of its tier for its minute. Hook up more than it can carry and they all slow down together.

It holds its Wicks at its tier's voltage, up to its rated current, and gives no more. Extracts burn much longer than buds: see [Extracts](entry:processing/extracts). The sums behind all of this are in [Ohm's Law at Work](entry:electronics/ohms_law).
