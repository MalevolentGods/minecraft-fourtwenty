A **Hookah** is a shared smoke and a power source in one. It needs a Cured Bud, a water bucket and charcoal; each burn uses a bud and a charcoal and lasts a minute. It starts itself when something on its Wicks needs power, and only then.

{table}
| Hookah | Tier | Power | Per burn |
| Hookah | Puff | 420 W | 25,200 Ws |
| Double Hose | Toke | 840 W | 50,400 Ws |
| Big Bertha | Rip | 1,680 W | 100,800 Ws |
| Grand Hookah | Blaze | 3,360 W | 201,600 Ws |
{/table}

A burn runs **four machines** of its tier for its minute. More machines than it can carry all slow down together.

It holds its Wicks at its tier's voltage up to its rated current and gives no more. Extracts burn much longer than buds: see [Extracts](entry:processing/extracts).
