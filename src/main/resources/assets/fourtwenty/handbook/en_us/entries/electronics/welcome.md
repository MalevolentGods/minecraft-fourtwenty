**Inspoweednetics** is electronics for people who'd rather be doing something else. It's real electronics, with the names changed to protect the innocent: the parts you'll build here behave like the parts in a real radio, a real computer, a real doorbell. Learn them here and you can read a real circuit diagram.

# What You'll Learn
1. **A little theory:** voltage, current, resistance and power, and what Weedcraft's strange units really mean.
2. **Binary:** how a computer counts on two fingers.
3. **Logic:** the gates that make decisions.
4. **The parts:** what's in the box.
5. **Six circuits to build**, from a light switch to a machine that adds.
6. **Going deeper:** the sums behind it all, from Ohm's law to how two transistors make a decision.
7. **The back of the book:** a translation table, so every part here can be looked up under its real name.

# Before You Start
You'll need power. A **Hookah** and a couple of rows of **Wicks** (see [Boundless Inspiration](entry:power/basics) and [Wicks and Wiring](entry:power/wiring)) are all it takes. Everything in this section runs on the first power tier, **Puff**.

Take your time. Nothing here can hurt you, and when you get it wrong, the worst that happens is a puff of smoke, some ash, and a badge.
