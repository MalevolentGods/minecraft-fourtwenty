The **Weed College** studies weed for the whole colony. Its **Weedologist** works through a board of studies of its own, separate from the University's research, and each finished study makes the colony's workers, farms or machines better at something.

Place the hut with the Build Tool, build it, and hire a Weedologist. Then open the hut's **Studies** tab, pick a branch and queue a study. Only the colony's owner and officers can change the queue.

# How a study goes
1. The Weedologist asks the warehouse for the study's supplies and carries them in.
2. They plant and harvest at the College's grow plots, run the crop through the Think Tank, and write it up at the desk. Each round is one unit of work.
3. About one round in ten is a bad batch and is done again. A study always gets there in the end.
4. When the last unit is written up, the colony is told.

A tier-1 study is 72 units, about 1.2 hours of one Weedologist's work, and each tier doubles it: a tier-6 study is about 38 hours. A second Weedologist halves the wait. A study moves only while a Weedologist is at work on it.

A study can come off the front of the queue until its supplies start arriving. After that it's under way and stays.

# The branches
**Chemistry** teaches the colony's dyers colours: the common ones first, then the hemp colours, one accord at a time. Its top studies cut the BI the colony's Terpinators draw: to half with Lean Extraction, a quarter with Full Spectrum, and nothing at all with Master Blend. Their speed never changes.

**Botany** breeds strains and sends their seeds to the warehouse: four seeds of each biome strain, two of each hybrid. Its yield studies give the colony's farmers an extra bud from a ripe weed crop: on one harvest in four with Soil Science, one in two with Selective Breeding, and every harvest with Master Grower.

**Engineering** works on the colony's machines:

{table}
| Study | What it does |
| Bench Habits | trimmers and curers work a quarter faster |
| Long Burn | Hookahs burn a quarter longer on each bud and charcoal |
| Deep Stash | Stashes hold half as much again |
| Grow Light Tuning | Grow Ops draw half their BI |
| Pressure Work | Rosin Rigs and Pheno Chambers work a quarter faster |
| Assembly Line | trimmers and curers work half again as fast |
| Boundless Grid | Hookahs burn twice as long |
{/table}

A later study in the same line replaces the earlier one; they don't add up. Only machines inside the colony's borders count.

**Glassworks** teaches the colony's Glassblower to make bongs, starting with the Glass Bong.

**Armory** teaches the colony's Blacksmith the Dab Cell, the Kush Suit and the Blazer.

**Textiles** studies patterns for the Tie Dye Shack.

# Colours
The College never teaches you a colour. It teaches the colony, and only the colony's own workers use what it learns. You still catch yours at the Inspiration Infuser.

If the colony's owner or an officer has already caught a colour, that colour's share of a study's supplies costs half. An accord you haven't discovered yourself stays unnamed on the board, even after the colony has studied it.
