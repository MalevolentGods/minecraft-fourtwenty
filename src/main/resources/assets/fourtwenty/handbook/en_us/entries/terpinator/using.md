The **Terpinator** turns weed into terpenes. It comes in two finishes, **Steel** and **Lab**: the same machine either way, so pick the one that suits your workshop.

1. Pick a colour you've caught at the [Inspiration Infuser](entry:colours/infuser).
2. Add Cured Buds. **Two vials from every bud.**
3. Press Start.

{image:scenes/workshop|A workshop: Inspiration Infuser, Terpinator, Rosin Rig and Pheno Chamber|wide}

# Which Weed
The sixteen common colours take any weed. Each hemp colour needs weed of its own **family**: the Terpinator tells you when the buds are the wrong kind.

# Power
It runs on BI through Wicks, or free with a high Weedcrafter within 21 blocks. With neither, it pauses and keeps its place.

# Production
Set a Terpinator for each colour you use most, feed them from a curer with hoppers, and draw the vials off underneath. A dye works never sleeps.
