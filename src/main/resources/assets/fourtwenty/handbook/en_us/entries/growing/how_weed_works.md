Every other page in this book is one stop on a single road. Here is the whole road, with the numbers, and a link at each stop to the page that goes deeper. If you only read one page of this chapter, make it this one.

# 1. The Seed
Everything starts as a **seed**. A seed carries a **strain** (Landrace, if it has no other name) and three **traits**: Potency, Yield and Length. The strain decides what a plant gives and what its smoke does. The traits decide how well. See [Strains](entry:growing/strains) and [Traits](entry:growing/traits).

You start with four Landrace seeds. After that, almost every new seed you get comes from [trimming](entry:growing/seeds).

# 2. The Plant
Plant it on wet farmland, in a [Pot Pot](entry:growing/pot_pots), or on a [Grow Op](entry:growing/grow_ops). It needs light of level 9 or more, and it grows about half as fast as wheat. Each grade of strain grows slower than the one below it; Grow Ops grow it faster (1.5x up to 8x). A plant in the dark or without power doesn't die. It waits. See [Planting](entry:growing/planting).

# 3. The Harvest
A ripe plant gives **Rough Buds** and its seed back. The whole plant comes up with the harvest, so you replant. How many buds depends on the strain's family (1 for Landrace, up to 5 for Golden God) and on the plant's **Yield** trait, which adds up to 3 more or takes 1 away (never fewer than one). The Aero Pod and Cosmic Kush Pod give half as many again. An unripe plant gives only its seed. See [Harvest](entry:growing/harvest).

# 4. Trimming
Rough Buds can't be smoked. **Trim** them, by hand at a Trim Tray or in a Snip Station and up, and each bud becomes a **Trimmed Bud**. Every bud trimmed has a small chance of dropping a **seed** of the same strain with the same traits: 14% for Landrace, down to 0.5% for Golden God. It's where almost all new seeds come from. See [Seeds](entry:growing/seeds) and [Trim and Cure](entry:processing/trim_and_cure).

# 5. Curing
Hang Trimmed Buds on a Curing Rack (or put them in a Dry Box and up). Five minutes later they are **Cured Buds**: the finished product, and what you smoke, bake, extract and turn into terpenes. Buds keep their strain and traits through every step.

# 6. Smoking
A Cured Bud becomes a joint, goes in a pipe or bong, burns in a Hookah, becomes an edible, or is pressed into [extracts](entry:processing/extracts) (Kief, Hash, Resin, Rosin, Oil), which make a pipe smoke stronger and longer. See [Smoking](entry:processing/smoking), [Edibles](entry:processing/edibles) and the [Vape Pen](entry:processing/vape_pen).

# 7. The High
Smoking gives you a **high**: your strain's effects, the world tinted its colour, and a timer. How long, and how strongly, depends on the strain's grade, the seed's traits, and the family. See [How a High Works](entry:processing/highs).

# 8. Why Being High Matters
While you are high, machines within 21 blocks of you run for free. A Hookah and its Wicks turn burning bud into Boundless Inspiration for every machine on the line. See [Power](entry:power/basics).

# 9. And Back to the Seed
Cross your best seeds in a [Pheno Chamber](entry:growing/breeding) and the child takes its parents' traits (and may drift a little better). Grow better plants, trim better seeds, cross them again. That's all of Weedcraft. The rest is detail, and the detail is in this book.
