Weed is a crop like wheat, with a few more needs, and it takes its time: about twice as long as other crops.

1. Till some dirt with a hoe, close to water so the farmland stays wet.
2. Plant a seed on the farmland.
3. Give it **light**: daylight, or a lamp nearby. In the dark (below light level 9) it simply waits.
4. Be patient. Bone meal hurries a Landrace along.
5. When the plant is fully grown, right-click it to **harvest**.

{item:fourtwenty:rough_bud|A Rough Bud, fresh off the plant}

A ripe plant gives you a **Rough Bud** (better strains give more) and its seed back. The plant itself comes up with the harvest, so replant the seed to keep your garden going.

Rough buds are sticky, leafy and not yet fit to smoke. To find out what to do with them, read [Your First Smoke](entry:getting_started/first_smoke).

For more on growing (better light, faster grows, and what grows where), see [Planting and Light](entry:growing/planting).
