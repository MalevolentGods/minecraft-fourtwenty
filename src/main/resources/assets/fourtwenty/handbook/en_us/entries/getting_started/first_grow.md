Weed is a crop, like wheat, only fussier and about half as fast.

1. Till some dirt with a hoe, close to water so the farmland stays wet.
2. Plant a seed on the farmland.
3. Give it **light**: daylight, or a lamp nearby. In the dark (below light level 9) it simply waits.
4. Wait. If waiting isn't your strong suit, bone meal hurries a Landrace along.
5. When the plant is fully grown, right-click it to **harvest**.

{image:scenes/first_grow|A first grow: wet farmland, water between the rows, and a lantern for the night|wide}

{item:fourtwenty:rough_bud|A Rough Bud, fresh off the plant}

A ripe plant gives you a **Rough Bud** (better strains give more) and its seed back. The whole plant comes up with the harvest, so put the seed straight back in the ground.

Rough Buds are sticky, leafy, and not yet fit to smoke. What to do about that is on the next page, [Your First Smoke](entry:getting_started/first_smoke).

For more on growing (better light, faster grows, and what grows where), see [Planting and Light](entry:growing/planting).
