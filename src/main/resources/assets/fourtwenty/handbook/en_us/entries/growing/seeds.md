A harvest gives each plant's seed back, and in plain farmland no more. To **grow your stock**, you trim.

Every time you trim a Rough Bud, a seed of its strain may fall out of the trimmings. The rarer the family, the rarer the seed:

{table}
| Family | Seed chance |
| Landrace | 14% |
| Neutrals, Warm | 15% |
| Green, Cool, Biome | 10% |
| Cross | 8% |
| Double Cross | 2% |
| Ultra | 1% |
| Golden | 0.5% |
{/table}

# Grow Ops Help
A plant grown in a Grow Op has an extra chance of a second seed at harvest: one chance in ten in a Closet Grow, rising to one in two in a Cosmic Kush Pod.

# Thrifty with Seeds
Don't smoke your way through your whole harvest while your seed stock is thin. Plant first, trim everything, and count your seeds before you roll.
