A harvest gives each plant's seed back, and in plain farmland nothing more. One seed in, one seed out: a garden that never gets any bigger. To **grow your stock**, you trim.

Every time you trim a Rough Bud, a seed of its strain may drop out of the trimmings. The rarer the family, the rarer the seed:

{table}
| Family | Seed chance |
| Landrace | 14% |
| Neutrals, Warm | 15% |
| Green, Cool, Biome | 10% |
| Cross | 8% |
| Double Cross | 2% |
| Ultra | 1% |
| Golden | 0.5% |
{/table}

# Grow Ops Help
A plant grown in a Grow Op has an extra chance of a second seed at harvest: one in ten in a Closet Grow, rising to one in two in a Cosmic Kush Pod.

# Trim Everything
Trim every Rough Bud you have, even the ones you don't need yet. An untrimmed bud is a seed you haven't found yet. A Golden God drops a seed one trim in two hundred, so if you're hoping for one, get comfortable.
