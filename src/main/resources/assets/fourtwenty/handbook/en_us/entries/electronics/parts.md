Every part has a **real-world twin**: its real name is shown quietly under its name on its label, and in the table at the back of this book.

# Wires
- **Wicks** carry power. **Hemp Thread** carries signals: a thin wire for logic, joining any part pin it touches.
- The **Eighth Ribbon** carries eight signal wires and a carry, together.
- **Nah, Skip Me, Bro** lets two wires cross without touching.

# Passive Parts
- **Resinator:** a resistor. It squeezes the flow.
- **Kief Cap:** a capacitor, which stores a little charge and gives it back.
- **Spliff Coil:** an inductor, which resists changes in the flow.
- **One-Hitter:** a diode, a one-way valve for current. **Zen Hitter:** a Zener diode, which holds a steady voltage.

# Switches
- **Toggle Joint:** a switch you flip.
- **Puff Button:** a button that closes for a moment.
- **Kill Switch:** a switch worked by a redstone signal (a relay).

# Logic: the Nugs
Gates (NOT, AND, OR, NAND, NOR, XOR, XNOR), a **Latch Nug** and a **Flip-Flop Nug** that remember, a **Buffer** and a **Tri-State Nug**. A Nug takes **VDD** (its power) from a Wick on top and **GND** (its ground) from a Wick underneath. Its output is in front. A gate with two inputs takes them on its left and right; a single-input one on its back.

# Time
The **Ticker** is a clock: a steady tick, tick, tick.

# Amplifiers and Switches You Can Steer
**Sativa FET** and **Indica FET:** transistors, switches worked by a voltage. The **Hype Amp** is an op-amp, making small signals large.

# Bytes
**Jar Nug** (a register that holds a byte), **Stacker Nug** (adds), **Scale Nug** (compares), **Blend Nug** (bitwise logic), **Sampler** and **Dealer** (a multiplexer and its opposite), **Puff-Pass** and **Pack** (shift registers), **Manyfier** and **Fewinator** (multiplier and divider), and **Numb Nug** to show a number.

# Light and Brains
A **Lit Bit** is a lamp, an LED: wire it to power and it glows white. A **Weedweeno**, a little computer, can tell every Lit Bit in range which colour to show.

# Tools
The **Twister** turns a part a quarter turn. The **Inspoweedometer** is your multimeter.
