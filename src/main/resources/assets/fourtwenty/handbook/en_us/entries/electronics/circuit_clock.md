title: Circuit 5: A Blinking Clock
icon: fourtwenty:ticker
terms: [Ticker, clock, oscillator]
---
**The goal:** a light that blinks by itself, steadily, forever.

**You'll need:** your [workbench](entry:electronics/board) and a **Ticker**.

1. Place the **Ticker** beside the board.
2. Give it power: a red Wick to its top, a black Wick to its bottom.
3. Watch it.

**What happens:** the Ticker's output turns on and off, once a second. Use it to step its period faster or slower (sneak to step back).

Wire its output into any of the earlier circuits, and the switch flips itself: a blinking light, a ticking counter, a heartbeat.

**The real thing:** a **clock**, or square-wave oscillator. Every computer has one, and it sets the pace for everything else.
