A **Hit Box** steps power between tiers, so a big source can run smaller machines without overloading them.

It's a transformer: two windings that never touch. The **front and back** are the high-voltage side; the **left and right** are the low-voltage side. Use the button to choose Toke to Puff, Rip to Toke or Blaze to Rip. A face only takes power when something on the other side can use it, and the Hit Box keeps 2% of whatever passes through, as heat.

# Long Lines
Every Wick has a little resistance, so a long line loses some power as heat. Stepping up to a higher tier for a long run, and back down at the far end, cuts that loss, but each Hit Box takes its 2%, so it only pays on very long runs: about 500 blocks or more. The sums, and the reason, are in [Sending Power a Long Way](entry:electronics/long_lines).
