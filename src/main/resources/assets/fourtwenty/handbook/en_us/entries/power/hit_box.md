A **Hit Box** steps power between tiers, so a big source can run smaller machines without overloading them.

It's a transformer with two windings that never touch. The **front and back** are the high-voltage side; the **left and right** are the low-voltage side. Use the button to choose Toke to Puff, Rip to Toke or Blaze to Rip. A face only takes power when something on the other side can use it, and 2% is lost on the way.

# Long Lines
Every Wick has a little resistance, so a long line loses some power as heat. At ten times the voltage, the same power needs a tenth of the current, so stepping up with a Hit Box at each end of a long run loses far less. The electricity company does exactly the same thing.
