Every Wick has a little squeeze, and flow through a squeeze makes heat: **W lost = Ig² × Φ**. Notice the square. Double the flow and a line loses **four times** as much power. That one square is why power grids look the way they do.

# Same Power, Less Flow
The same power can travel as a big push and a small flow, or a small push and a big flow: 420 W is 4.2 Kb × 100 Ig, or 42 Kb × 10 Ig. Down the same wire, the second loses a **hundredth** as much: a tenth of the flow, squared. That's why real power lines run at hundreds of thousands of volts, and only step down near your door.

# The Transformer
A transformer is two coils of wire wound on one iron core, never touching. With ten turns on one coil for every one on the other, it trades push for flow, ten to one: ten times the push on one side, a tenth of the flow, and the same power on both, less a little heat. The [Hit Box](entry:power/hit_box) is a transformer.

# The Catch, in Real Life
A real transformer only works on **alternating current** (AC), current that keeps reversing direction, because it works through a *changing* magnetic field, and a steady flow doesn't change. That's why the real grid runs on AC. BI is **direct current** (DC): it only ever flows one way. The Hit Box works on it anyway. Nobody knows how. Phu objects to this regularly, in writing.

# The Catch, in Weedcraft
Weedcraft's higher-tier Wicks are thinner for their tier: a block of Rolled Wick has fifty times the squeeze of a block of Hemp. So carrying a Hookah's 420 W at Toke instead of Puff only halves the loss, and each Hit Box keeps 2%. In numbers: at a full Hookah's flow, Hemp Wick loses about 0.06 W per block, and the same power on Rolled Wick about 0.03 W, while two Hit Boxes cost about 17 W between them. Stepping up only pays on runs of more than about **500 blocks**.

For anything shorter, run Hemp and stop worrying: a 50-block line loses about 3 W of a Hookah's 420.
